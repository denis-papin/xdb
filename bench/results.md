# xdb POC benchmark results

Generated by `xdb-bench` in 66.8 s wall time. 259 runs; all serializable (xdb-checker): **yes**; all workload invariants hold: **yes**.

Simulated cluster (deterministic, seed 42): 25 us one-way latency, 3 us mean jitter, 30 us fsync, 2 us per micro-instruction, 5 us coordinator overhead per call. Commit waits for a majority of replicas (leader included): 2 replicas = both, 3 = 2, 4 = 3. Latencies are simulated microseconds; throughput is committed calls per simulated second. `restarts` = transaction-block restarts (wounded / died / refresh / stage); `aborts/commit` = restarts per successful call. Full per-run data: `results.csv`.
